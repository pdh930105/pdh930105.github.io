Research Summary

I am a PhD student focusing on efficient deep learning systems, with an emphasis on low-bit quantization and MCU-level inference. My research aims to bridge the gap between algorithm design and hardware efficiency, enabling deep neural networks to run on highly resource-constrained environments.

Specifically, I work on:

  • Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) for CNNs and LLMs
  • Sub-4-bit / Binary neural networks
  • MCU-friendly convolution acceleration (e.g., CMSIS-NN, RISC-V)
  • Memory-efficient inference and error robustness

My long-term goal is to design hardware-aware AI systems that achieve state-of-the-art performance under extreme constraints.
